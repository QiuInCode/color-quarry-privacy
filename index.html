Color Quarry (翻滚清山) is operated by an individual developer. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad game and this policy website. For privacy questions, contact fangqiuuu123@gmail.com.

Game data on your device

The game stores level progress, stars, unfinished boards, earned coins, tool inventory, hint balances, and preferences on your device. These records support continued play and save restoration. The current release operates with local game saves.

If you choose an age range, the game stores one of three values on your device, under 18, 18 or older, or unspecified. This controls eligibility for optional video hints. You can change the choice in Settings.

Optional statistics and diagnostic records

Optional game statistics start disabled. If you enable them, the game keeps a local queue of events such as level starts and results, hints, stars, and purchase or restore outcomes. A random installation identifier associates these local events. The queue holds at most 200 events for up to 7 days. Withdrawing statistics consent clears this queue and identifier. The current release processes this queue locally.

A separate optional playtest record can keep the latest 1,000 diagnostic entries on your device. You can choose to copy and send this record for support. If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. Please share only details needed for the issue.

Optional rewarded advertising

Video hints are available to players who confirm they are 18 or older and whose ad requests are permitted through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Players under 18 or with an unspecified age use the game's free hints, earned reserve hints, and failure refills. Verified permanent ad-removal owners can claim the included extra hints directly within the per-level limit.

The game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and requests non-personalized ads. When the service is active, Google may process an IP address, device or app identifiers, diagnostic and performance data, ad data, and interactions for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate approximate location. Consent choices and regional requirements can affect processing. Google's SDK applies the consent settings, and you can revisit available privacy options in the game's Settings.

More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's iOS advertising data disclosure.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases and payment information. The game receives product and verified transaction information to deliver and restore permanent ad removal and block skins. Verified entitlements are cached securely on the device for offline use and refreshed through Apple's purchase services. Apple handles purchase approval and refund requests.

Apple describes its processing in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your choices and retention

You can change age range, optional statistics, and available advertising privacy choices in Settings. Data and Support lets you clear the current game entry's progress and backup copies. Local progress and preferences are retained for continued use until you change or clear the relevant records. The statistics queue follows the limits above. Purchase restoration follows your Apple account and Apple's transaction records.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a copy of personal information that we hold, and to withdraw consent or contact your local privacy authority. Send requests concerning support correspondence to the email above. Data held only on your device is managed through the game and device controls. For data handled by Apple or Google, their privacy resources describe the available controls and request procedures.

International processing and this website

Apple and Google may process service data in the countries and under the safeguards described in their privacy policies. This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and technical request information to provide and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Policy updates

Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes to data use will also be communicated in the game where appropriate.

隐私政策

生效日期为2026年11月7日

翻滚清山（Color Quarry）由个人开发者运营。本政策适用于iPhone、iPad游戏及本隐私网站。隐私问题请联系 fangqiuuu123@gmail.com。

保存在设备上的游戏数据

游戏在设备上保存关卡进度、星级、残局、金币、道具库存、提示余额和操作偏好，用于继续游玩与恢复存档。当前版本使用本机游戏存档。

选择年龄范围后，游戏仅在本机保存“未满18岁”“18岁及以上”或“未选择”三种范围之一，用于确定自愿视频提示资格。可在设置中修改。

自愿统计与诊断记录

可选游戏统计默认关闭。主动开启后，游戏在本机记录关卡开始与结果、提示、星级、购买和恢复结果等事件，并使用随机安装标识关联这些本机事件。队列最多200条，最长保留7天。撤回统计同意后，队列和随机安装标识会被清除。当前版本仅在本机处理该队列。

另有默认关闭的试玩记录，可在本机保留最近1,000条诊断条目。你可主动复制并发送记录以寻求支持。收到你主动发送的邮件后，我们使用其中的信息处理你的请求，请仅提供排查问题所需的信息。

自愿激励广告

视频提示面向已确认18岁及以上、且Google UMP允许请求广告的玩家。未满18岁或未选择年龄时，可使用游戏免费提示、备用提示和失败补给。持有经商店验证的永久免广告权益时，可按每关额度直接领取所含额外提示。

游戏通过Google AdMob提供自愿激励广告，请求非个性化广告。服务启用后，Google可能处理IP地址、设备或应用标识、诊断与性能数据、广告数据及互动信息，用于投放、衡量和反作弊。IP地址可能用于估计大致位置。同意选择与地区要求会影响数据处理，Google SDK执行相关同意设置。可从游戏设置重新打开可用的广告隐私选项。

详情见Google隐私政策及Google iOS广告数据披露。

购买与恢复

Apple处理App Store购买和付款信息。游戏取得商品及已验证交易信息，用于发放和恢复永久免广告与方块皮肤。已验证权益安全缓存在设备上以支持离线使用，并通过Apple购买服务刷新。购买批准和退款申请由Apple处理。详情见Apple隐私政策。

你的选择与保留期限

可从设置修改年龄范围、可选统计和可用的广告隐私选择。“数据与支持”提供清空当前游戏入口进度及备份的操作。本机进度与偏好为继续使用而保留，直到你修改或清除相应记录。统计队列采用上文的条数与时间限制。购买恢复依据Apple账户及其交易记录。

根据所在地规定，你可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制处理、反对处理、取得个人信息副本、撤回同意或向当地隐私主管机关反映问题的权利。涉及支持邮件的信息请求可发送至上述邮箱。仅保存在设备上的数据可通过游戏及设备设置管理。Apple或Google处理的数据，其隐私资源提供相应控制与申请方式。

跨境处理与本网站

Apple和Google可能按各自隐私政策所述地区和保障措施处理服务数据。本网站由GitHub Pages托管，GitHub可能处理访问者IP地址及技术请求信息，以提供和保障服务。详情见GitHub隐私声明。

政策更新
