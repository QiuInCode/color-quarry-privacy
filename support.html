Contact support

Email fangqiuuu123@gmail.com to contact the individual developer. For a gameplay issue, include the level number, app version, device model, system version, and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots are optional. Share only information needed to explain the issue.

Purchases and restoration

Permanent ad removal, Celadon, Satin Gold, and the bundle are one-time non-consumable purchases. Open Settings and choose Restore Purchases while using the Apple account that made the purchase. Purchased block skins can be selected in Settings, then Block Skins. Apple reviews purchase refunds through Report a Problem.

Hints and saved progress

Each level has its own free hint allowance. Eligible video rewards and direct claims share a total limit of two extra hints per level. Permanent ad removal lets you claim these hints directly. Earned reserve hints and failure refills follow the game rules. Settings contains age range and privacy choices.

Game progress is stored on the device. Data and Support provides controls for the current game entry's progress and backup copies. Please contact support before clearing records if you need help diagnosing a save problem.

个人开发者的联系邮箱为 fangqiuuu123@gmail.com。反馈玩法问题时，请提供关卡编号、App版本、设备型号、系统版本和复现步骤，可按需附上截图。请仅提供说明问题所需的信息。

购买与恢复

永久免广告、青瓷、缎金及超值包均为一次购买的非消耗型商品。使用购买时的Apple账户，在设置中选择“恢复购买”。已购皮肤可从设置中的“方块皮肤”选用。退款申请由Apple通过报告问题页面处理。

提示与存档

各关具有独立免费提示额度。符合资格的视频奖励和直接领取共用每关累计两次的额外上限，永久免广告可以直接领取。备用提示和失败补给按游戏规则取得，年龄范围和隐私选择可从设置调整。

游戏进度保存在设备上，“数据与支持”提供当前游戏入口进度与备份的管理操作。如需排查存档问题，请在清除记录前先联系支持。
